Mogra Plant — Jasminum sambac
Mogra, commonly known as Arabian Jasmine, is a fragrant flowering plant widely grown in India. It is admired for its pure white flowers and calming aroma. 🌿 Estimated Oxygen Release Rate: Mogra plants help improve air quality by releasing oxygen and supporting a healthy environment. 🌍 Carbon Sequestration Rate: A healthy Mogra plant can absorb approximately 0.08–0.15 kg of CO₂ per year. ✨ Attractive & Interesting Uses: 1️⃣ Aromatic & Ornamental Plant: Mogra flowers are popular for garlands, hair decoration, and beautifying homes and temples. 2️⃣ Medicinal Uses: Traditionally, Mogra flowers are used to relieve stress, improve sleep quality, and calm the mind. 3️⃣ Cultural & Religious Importance: Mogra is widely used in religious rituals and festivals across India. 🌸 Fun Fact: Mogra flowers bloom at night and release their strongest fragrance after sunset.
